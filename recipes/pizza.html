Pizza

Pizza is a popular dish made with a round, flat dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of ingredients such as vegetables, meat, and herbs. It is baked in an oven until the crust becomes crispy and the cheese melts. Pizza comes in many flavors and is enjoyed around the world as a delicious meal for lunch, dinner, or sharing with friends and family.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Warm water
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Oregano
  • Toppings of your choice, such as onions, peppers, mushrooms, or meat

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, warm water, and olive oil in a bowl.
  2. Knead the dough until smooth, then cover it and let it rise until doubled in size.
  3. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  4. Roll the dough into a round shape and place it on a baking tray.
  5. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  6. Add mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.
  7. Bake for about 12–18 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  8. Let the pizza cool slightly, slice it, and serve.
