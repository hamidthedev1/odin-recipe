Pizza

Pizza is a popular dish made with a round, flat dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of ingredients such as vegetables, meat, and herbs. It is baked in an oven until the crust becomes crispy and the cheese melts. Pizza comes in many flavors and is enjoyed around the world as a delicious meal for lunch, dinner, or sharing with friends and family.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Warm water
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Oregano
  • Toppings of your choice, such as onions, peppers, mushrooms, or meat
