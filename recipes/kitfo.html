Kitfo

Kitfo is a traditional Ethiopian dish made from finely minced raw beef seasoned with Ethiopian spices and clarified butter known as niter kibbeh. It is often flavored with mitmita, a spicy Ethiopian seasoning that gives it a distinctive taste. Kitfo can be served raw, lightly cooked, or fully cooked, depending on personal preference. It is commonly enjoyed with kocho, injera, and ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese), making it a special dish in Ethiopian cuisine.

Ingredients

  • Finely minced beef
  • Niter kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced butter)
  • Mitmita (Ethiopian spicy seasoning)
  • Cardamom or other traditional spices (optional)
  • Salt
  • Ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese), for serving (optional)
  • Kocho or injera, for serving

Steps

  1. Finely mince the beef and prepare the niter kibbeh, mitmita, and salt.
  2. Gently warm the niter kibbeh in a pan over low heat.
  3. Add the mitmita and salt, then stir to combine.
  4. Add the minced beef and mix thoroughly with the seasoned butter.
  5. Cook the beef thoroughly, stirring regularly. For safety, ground beef should reach 71°C (160°F).
  6. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.
  7. Serve with kocho or injera and ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese).
