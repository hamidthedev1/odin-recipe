Ethiopan Shiro

Shiro is a traditional Ethiopian dish made from powdered chickpeas or broad beans mixed with water, onions, garlic, tomatoes, and Ethiopian spices. It is slowly cooked into a smooth, thick stew with a rich and flavorful taste. Shiro is commonly served hot with injera and is a popular meal in Ethiopian households, enjoyed for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

    Shiro powder (ground chickpeas or broad beans) Onion Garlic Tomatoes (optional) Berbere spice Niter kibbeh or cooking oil Water Salt

Steps

  1. Finely chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Heat niter kibbeh or cooking oil in a pot over medium heat.
  3. Add the onion and cook until soft.
  4. Add the garlic, tomatoes if using, and berbere spice. Stir for a few minutes.
  5. Gradually add water while stirring to prevent lumps.
  6. Slowly add the shiro powder and continue stirring until the mixture becomes smooth.
  7. Simmer gently until the stew thickens and the shiro powder is fully cooked. Add more water if necessary.
  8. Season with salt and serve hot with injera.
